A born hybrid

As someone of mixed heritage, you could say Joel was born a hybrid.

Half analytical thinker, half creative tinkerer. During his decade as a Creative Director in advertising, he straddled the line between strategy and execution, delivering thoughtful, award-winning work for brands like Netflix, Under Armour, Nike, and the NFL.

Today as a Product Designer at Yelp, he continues to blur traditional boundaries by blending holistic vision thinking with an obsession for visual craft. From coding functional vision prototypes to fine-tuning micro-animations and architecting product frameworks, no problem is too big or small.

Joel’s hybrid nature extends far beyond his career. Part gamer, part athlete, and part extroverted introvert, he’s a homebody who loves to travel and a Type A planner who likes to get his hands dirty sculpting clay. He has never quite fit neatly into a single box—which is probably why he has spent most of his life breaking out of them.
